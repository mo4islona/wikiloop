## 10. Language and tone

Write all text in this repo in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (STE):

- Use only STE approved words, with their approved meanings. You can use technical names
  and technical verbs: metric names, namespaces, commands, `deploy`, `rollback`.
- Write a maximum of 20 words in an instruction and 25 words in a descriptive sentence.
- Write one instruction in each sentence. Use the imperative for instructions.
- Use the active voice. Use only the present, simple past and simple future tense.
- Do not omit the articles "the" and "a".
- Write a maximum of three words in a noun cluster.
- Use a vertical list for a sequence of steps or a group of conditions.

Do not change literal text: code, error messages, log lines, and the phrases that a person
types, for example in a `description`. Write in English only. Give the mechanism, not the
narrative. Put metric names, namespaces and paths in backticks, exactly as the system shows them.

## 5a. What stays out

The wiki is internal, but its text reaches every laptop with the plugin. Do not write
customer names, contract terms or the contents of a security finding. Link the ticket that
holds them instead.
